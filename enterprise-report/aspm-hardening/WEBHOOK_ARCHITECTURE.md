# Durable outbound webhooks

WebhookSubscription belongs to one Product; changing scope requires a new subscription. WebhookEvent stores an immutable payload, Product, stable UUID and subscription revision snapshot. PostgreSQL triggers capture actual repository/commit creation, scan/report transitions and meaningful Finding field changes, including QuerySet.update and concurrent writers. Business rollback also rolls back the event. Updates that leave tracked fields unchanged produce no event. Capture is conditional on matching enabled subscriptions. Disabling the application feature pauses management/dispatch; archive/disable subscriptions to stop capture.

The existing ingestion dispatcher expands at most 100 events and schedules at most 100 deliveries per tick. It locks subscription admission once per batch and computes pending counts together. Pending capacity is configurable per subscription; overflow is explicitly dead-lettered with capacity_limit rather than rolling back ingestion. Each event/subscription pair is unique. A dispatch lease recovers broker publication gaps independently of retry backoff. Session locks fence individual deliveries across workers; HTTP occurs outside row transactions.

Stable event/delivery IDs support receiver deduplication. Delivery is at least once: a worker may die after a successful HTTP send and before committing the result. Attempts and interrupted attempts remain visible. Exponential capped backoff with jitter, timeout, per-endpoint rate, bounded retry cycles, manual replay, archive/disable and secret rotation are implemented. Rotation/config changes revoke queued old revisions; an already-started request cannot be retracted. Store durable delivery/event history according to organizational retention and monitor dead letters.
